Good to know

Questions? Start here.

More event details will be shared with registered students when they are confirmed.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Ideas need designers, researchers, storytellers, and problem-solvers too. Bring the skills you have and be ready to learn.

Can I register without a team?

Yes. Choose “I'm looking for teammates” or “I'd like to join solo” in the form. Team formation details can be shared with participants later.

When and where will it happen?

The event date and venue have not been announced yet. Check the updates shared by your institution after registering your interest.

Does this form confirm my registration?

This website is a front-end demo and does not submit data to an organizer. A real registration confirmation requires a connected form service or server.
